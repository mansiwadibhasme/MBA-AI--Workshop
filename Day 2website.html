Our Craft & Materials

Honest fibers, archival sizing, and unlacquered brass.

Every sheet we bind and every barrel we turn respects the physical dialogue between hand, ink, and paper. We partner directly with family-run mills in Echizen and small-batch machine shops in Nagano.

01. Sustainable Forestry & Kozo Fiber

FSC-certified Nordic wood pulp blended with regenerative Japanese mulberry (kozo) and cotton linters for supple tear strength.

02. Acid-Free Paper Milling

Neutral-pH internal sizing tested with broad wet fountain pen nibs—preserving ink sheen and shading for over a century without yellowing.

03. Precision Brass Machining

Lathe-turned from solid C36000 brass rod with micro-threaded caps. Left raw to record the quiet patina of your daily grip.
